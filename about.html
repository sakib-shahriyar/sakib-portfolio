Biography

I am Sakib Shahriyar Pathan, Lecturer, Department of ICT at Gosinga Alim Madrasah, Bangladesh. I hold an MSc in Computer Science & Engineering from BUET and a BSc in Computer Science & Engineering from Southeast University.

Over 8 years of teaching, I have taught HSC Physics and ICT, SSC Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Higher Mathematics, and O/A Level ICT and Computer Science. That range taught me one thing: students do not struggle because a subject is hard — they struggle because it was never explained simply.

My teaching philosophy is straightforward: make ICT simple — memorise less, understand more. Every note I write and every lecture I plan starts from the concept, builds up with real examples, and only then touches the exam question. Understanding first; marks follow.
