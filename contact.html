FAQ

Questions from parents and students

The answers to what I am asked most often. Anything else, send a message above.

How do I join an HSC ICT batch?

Go to the Courses page, choose Batch 2027 or Batch 2028, and tap Join on WhatsApp. Classes are held online on Google Meet on Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday, and admission is open now.

How do I get the complete notes for a chapter?

Open the Notes page and preview the first 4 pages of any document free. For the complete copy, send a message through the form above or on WhatsApp, and I will send it to you personally.

Are the practice tests free?

Yes. The Practice page is free for everyone: chapter-wise tests and real board papers, a timer, instant scores, and explanations for the questions you get wrong.

Which levels and subjects do you teach?

HSC ICT; SSC Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Higher Mathematics; and Cambridge O Level, Edexcel IGCSE and A Level ICT and Computer Science.

Where are the classes held?

The current HSC ICT batches are fully online on Google Meet, so you can join from anywhere. For O Level, IGCSE and A Level arrangements, message me on WhatsApp.

How will I know when a new video lecture is published?

On the Lectures page, fill in the Notify me box with your name, batch, chapter and WhatsApp number. You will get a WhatsApp message when a new video for your chapter comes out.

What are the batch fees?

Fees are shared on WhatsApp rather than listed here. Send a message naming the batch you are interested in, and I will reply with the details.
